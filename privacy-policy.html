Personal Information Protection Policy

2
Purpose of Use of Personal Information

Otoz.Ai will use Personal Information within the scope of the purposes provided in the Purpose of Use. The Purpose of Use is specifically defined and made clear to our customers, and is announced on our website as shown below. Otoz.Ai will make an effort to limit the Purpose of Use according to the situation in which the information is obtained.

  1. 1Achieving the Purpose of Use of our service and business.
  2. 2Notices of service, etc. to its customers.
  3. 3Giving notice to shareholders, provision of various types of information and shareholder management.
  4. 4Exercise of rights or performance of obligations based on Act of Japan and other relevant laws and ordinances.
  5. 5Responding to inquiries, requests, etc. from its customers.
  6. 6Carrying out operations incidental to (1) through (5) above and operations to properly and smoothly manage the business of Otoz.Ai.

When handling Personal Information beyond the scope necessary for achieving the Purpose of Use, Otoz.Ai will seek customer's consent except for cases prescribed under items in Paragraph 3 of Article 16 of the Privacy Act.

3
Provision of Personal Data to Third Parties

Otoz.Ai will not provide Personal Information to a third party without the customer's consent, unless where any of the following applies:

  1. 1Required under laws or ordinances.
  2. 2Required to protect human life, body or asset (including that of legal entities) and difficult to obtain the approval by the customer him/herself.
  3. 3Made to a consignee within the scope necessary for carrying out our business.
  4. 4To use Personal Information jointly with a group company or a business partner of Otoz.Ai within the scope of the intended use.
  5. 5To provide, in an unrecoverable format, Personal Information in a format that does not allow easy identification or recognition of the corresponding individual.

When handling Personal Information beyond the scope necessary for achieving the Purpose of Use, Otoz.Ai will seek customer's consent except for cases prescribed under items in Paragraph 3 of Article 16 of the Privacy Act.

4
Disclaimer Concerning Provision to Third Parties

Otoz.Ai will not be responsible for the acquisition of Personal Information by a third party where any of the following applies:

  1. 1A User indicates his/her own Personal Information to a specific company, using any function under the Services or by other means.
  2. 2A User is unexpectedly identified by information entered by the User under the Services.
  3. 3A User provides Personal Information to, and the User's Personal Information is used at, an external site linked by the Services.
  4. 4Anyone other than a User obtains the User's information (ID, password, etc.) which can identify the User as an individual.
  5. 5Additionally to above, Personal Information is leaked by computer viruses or similar causes.
5

Otoz.Ai may consign all or part of our customer's Personal Information handling operation.
